# v0.0.4 release checklist

"THE BOTS REMEMBER". The release the three miners become characters in: they
carry attitudes between sessions, talk to each other and to the player, and
hold grudges. See `docs/rfcs/0003-digs-bot-memory-and-conversation.md`.

Two things about this release change how it has to be checked.

**Most of the headline feature cannot be tested inside one match.** Memory,
the inbox and contract drift are only visible across a quit-and-relaunch
cycle, and pacing and interruption are judged by ear. No automated lane
covers any of it. The checkpoints that do are VOX-QA-089 through 093 and 096
through 100, and they are release-blocking rather than nice to have.

**The corroborating build lanes are gone.** Through v0.0.3 a Rust boundary
and a C++98 translation unit independently exercised the core. v0.0.4 is ISO
C90 only, so the portable-core CI matrix is now the entire mechanical
portability claim. Treat a red lane there as more serious than it used to be.

## Version and provenance

- [ ] `VERSION` reads `0.0.4` and `tools/vox-version-check.sh` passes from a clean checkout.
- [ ] `tools/version-exceptions.txt` contains no rule that exempts a surface describing *this* build rather than project history.
- [ ] The generated `vox/vox_version.h` in the release build matches `VERSION`, and the shipped binary's window title reads `DIGS v0.0.4`.
- [ ] Release assets for v0.0.3 are untouched. The v0.0.4 tag uses `.github/workflows/publish-release.yml`; the v0.0.3 legacy-addendum workflow is not reused and not re-run.

## Correctness and determinism

- [ ] ABI 10 agrees across every first-party translation unit; settings schemas 1 through 5 all migrate, and the accepted-version list names every readable schema.
- [ ] Chronicle format 1 round-trips, repairs a checksum failure from the last good copy, raises TAMPERED, and treats an absent file as a first launch rather than an error.
- [ ] **The saved layer stays out of the canonical hash.** `--load-self-test 600` prints `1acec253` on a pref path with a long history and on a fresh one, and `test_the_clock_stays_out_of_the_hash` passes.
- [ ] No wall-clock value reaches any digest the canonical hash consumes. `launch_counter` and `elapsed_coarse` remain excluded from `vox_digs_memory_hash`, with the reason recorded at the exclusion site.
- [ ] `vox_digs_match_init` still initialises every field explicitly; there is no `memset`, and `test_init_leaves_nothing_uninitialised` passes.
- [ ] Line-id packing is sound: `digs_lines_stride_is_sound` passes, and no set exceeds the stride or pushes an id past `vox_u16`.
- [ ] Identical input streams produce identical hashes at 15/30/60/90/120/144/Unlimited presentation and through scalar/NASM paths.
- [ ] Simulation hashes are invariant across `-O0`, `-O1`, `-O2` and `-Os`.
- [ ] Everything v0.0.3 proved about collision recovery, swept anatomy hits, debris, rope, rail and steampack still holds: those tests are unchanged and still pass.

## The miners

- [ ] Bots breach walls with a cutting tool rather than grinding against terrain; no bot stands still against a wall for more than a few seconds.
- [ ] **Memory survives a relaunch.** A miner antagonised in one session opens colder in the next, and the two left alone do not (VOX-QA-089).
- [ ] The inbox fills after time away with attributed messages in each miner's own voice, reads `NO MAIL TODAY` when empty, and clears unread marks on open (VOX-QA-090, 091).
- [ ] The Options memory reset states what it destroys, requires a second deliberate press, and leaves history intact if backed out of (VOX-QA-093).
- [ ] Tone changes are hysteretic: no pair flickers between tones while sitting on a boundary.

## The conversation

- [ ] The player character never speaks unprompted, and every bark press produces exactly one line that fits the situation. No press is silently swallowed (VOX-QA-097).
- [ ] At most one speech bubble is on screen at any moment, including during heavy fighting (VOX-QA-096).
- [ ] A match with bots contains several exchanges even when the player never barks, and pressing bark visibly raises how much the bots say. Neither silence nor wall-to-wall chatter (VOX-QA-100).
- [ ] Interruption is a trait, not a rule: RIVET nearly always lets the player finish, CINDER often does not, FLAMEY is inconsistent, and no archetype interrupts every time (VOX-QA-099).
- [ ] No reply is orphaned. A patient archetype's reply is clamped to the exchange it belongs to and is actually spoken.
- [ ] Playing alone, repeated barks build one train of thought rather than restarting from unrelated openers, and do not repeat lines (VOX-QA-098).
- [ ] Aiming at a miner makes it the subject of the line and the most likely to answer.
- [ ] No line reads as assembled. Every line comes from the written corpus, and an unwritten combination produces silence rather than a blank bubble.
- [ ] The warm pools carry their weight: de-escalation reads as clearly as escalation, with no relationship UI to fall back on.

## Interface

- [ ] `--menu-self-test` passes across all screens, and nothing draws over a title or footer on any of them (VOX-QA-102).
- [ ] Long log lines wrap with indented continuations under the speaker's name; nothing is truncated (VOX-QA-101).
- [ ] Screens with more entries than fit show a scrollbar, scroll the selection into view for keyboard and pad, and hit-test the mouse against the row actually under the pointer after scrolling.
- [ ] Each miner's messages are colour-coded and the player's own are white.

- [ ] The portable 600-tick four-miner load regression reproduces fired 43, explosions 16, crushes 1, effects 474, awake 10754, and state hash `1acec253` without a host timing assertion.
- [ ] Four-miner explosive soak meets 5 ms average, 8 ms p95, and 16.67 ms max simulation budgets on the named power-saver test bench. **Not yet recorded for v0.0.4** — the compatibility matrix carries this as PLANNED and it must be closed or explicitly stated as unproven before tagging.
- [ ] Laptop Mode leaves authoritative rules, FX counts, hashes, bot decisions, and material results identical to normal presentation.
- [ ] Startup qualification gates frame caps the local machine cannot sustain; it never silently changes simulation rate.
- [ ] Device-clock audio has no underrun, pitch, duration, or speech-timing change across irregular render cadence and every cap.
- [ ] Off/Low/Normal/Heavy haptics differ measurably and remain local/distance-weighted; Switch Pro USB and Bluetooth receive separate manual evidence.
- [ ] Visible-region rendering preserves adjacent close-zoom voxels and exposes no black outside the world at any camera edge.

## Packaging and publication

- [ ] Strict ISO C90 warnings-as-errors, native CTest, and scalar/NASM parity gates pass from a clean checkout. There is no cargo lane; its absence is intentional, not an omission.
- [ ] ASan and UBSan suites pass, with leak detection disabled only for the ptraced ASan environment.
- [ ] Windows x64, macOS Intel, and Linux i686 portable-core CI lanes are green on the frozen commit. These are now the only mechanical ISO C portability evidence.
- [ ] The Win32 legacy PE32 host still builds and passes `digs_legacy_headless_scenario`. It compiles `vox_sdl_ui.c`, which this release changed.
- [ ] **The playable payload is at or below 1,474,560 bytes.** `tools/vox-size-report.sh` is enforced inside `tools/package-linux-demo.sh`, so a package that exceeds it fails rather than ships.
- [ ] Smoke, benchmark, input, settings, camera, audio, bark, chronicle, menu, haptic-mixer, and the 600-tick deterministic load self-test are captured in every package.
- [ ] The QA workbook regenerates byte-identically under CPython 3.12.3 / openpyxl 3.1.2 / lxml 5.2.1, and `--check` passes inside the packager.
- [ ] The easy-run Linux bundle, launcher, `qa/V0.0.4-QUICK-FEEDBACK.txt`, `CG-README.TXT`, QA workbook/cockpit, Corresponding Source, SPDX SBOM, and SHA256SUMS agree on v0.0.4.
- [ ] Manual one- and two-human matches cover every map/landform, rising lava, bots, all eleven weapons, pause/results/restart, and clean shutdown.
- [ ] Compatibility claims remain evidence-scoped; no GPU, historical-OS, or untested controller claim is inferred. Both PLANNED rows in the v0.0.4 matrix section are closed or restated as unproven.
- [ ] The accepted phase commits are merged to protected `main`, pushed to `origin`, and the final clean commit is used for the release tag.

## Known deliberate exceptions

Recorded so a future reader does not "fix" them back:

- **The Options memory reset exists in the released build.** RFC 0003's design
  intent is that the history between the player and these three is theirs and
  unrepeatable, which a reset undoes. It is present at the lead's explicit
  request, made after the trade-off was put to them, behind a two-press
  confirm.
- **There is no relationship UI.** No nameplate tint, no standings screen, no
  addressing markers. This is the decision most likely to need revisiting: if
  relationships read as invisible in play, the nameplate tint is the cheapest
  remedy and should be reconsidered before anything more elaborate.
