# v0.0.3 release checklist

## Correctness and determinism

- [ ] ABI 9 agrees across C/C++ and Rust; settings schemas 1 and 2 migrate to 3 and future schemas remain untouched.
- [ ] Embedded-body tests prove bounded recovery with no unexplained spawn, score, health, anatomy, or position jump.
- [ ] Swept projectile/melee/blast tests hit every visible anatomy part, retain cover ordering, and never tunnel or double-hit.
- [ ] Shield blocks, delayed attribution, bleeding, dismemberment, lava, self-damage, and environmental deaths score exactly once.
- [ ] Repeated debris/gore impacts produce no vertical needles, airborne TTL expiry creates no terrain, and loose matter obeys support/gravity.
- [ ] Hold and Toggle rope modes cover cast, miss, occlusion, reel, wrap, anchor loss, source switch, death, and respawn.
- [ ] Rope-hook contact sets an unshielded enemy to exactly 1 HP without death/anatomy damage; spawn shields block all health, knockback, and attribution.
- [ ] Charged rail tests cover charge/release, energy loss, soft terrain, stone/bedrock, multiple miners, exact anatomy, recoil, cooldown, and AI use.
- [ ] Steampack trajectories prove controllable lift, capped momentum, glide, fuel depletion, and recharge at fixed 60 Hz.
- [ ] Identical input streams produce identical hashes at 15/30/60/90/120/144/Unlimited presentation and through scalar/NASM paths.

## Performance and presentation

- [ ] Sleeping reaction cells are skipped and a bounded active frontier prevents same-pass world wake cascades.
- [ ] The portable 600-tick four-miner load regression reproduces canonical activity and state hash `f3924b81` without a host timing assertion.
- [ ] Four-miner explosive soak meets 5 ms average, 8 ms p95, and 16.67 ms max simulation budgets on the named power-saver test bench.
- [ ] Visible-region/native-scene rendering preserves adjacent close-zoom voxels and exposes no black outside the world at any camera edge.
- [ ] Laptop Mode leaves authoritative rules, FX counts, hashes, bot decisions, and material results identical to normal presentation.
- [ ] Startup qualification gates frame caps the local machine cannot sustain; it never silently changes simulation rate.
- [ ] Device-clock audio has no underrun, pitch, duration, or speech-timing change across irregular render cadence and every cap.
- [ ] Off/Low/Normal/Heavy haptics differ measurably and remain local/distance-weighted for fire, rope, rail, hits, explosions, kills, and collapse.
- [ ] Nintendo, Xbox, PlayStation, and generic prompts display the current binding; Switch Pro USB and Bluetooth receive separate manual evidence.
- [ ] Steampack flame/exhaust and rail afterimage receive Lightfield illumination without mutating the match world or hash.
- [ ] Dummy Mode affects only local human bark cooldown; bot/global spacing and announcer protection remain intact.

## Packaging and publication

- [ ] Strict C89/C++98 warnings-as-errors, native CTest, Cargo, scalar/NASM parity, ASan, and UBSan gates pass from a clean checkout.
- [ ] Windows x64, macOS Intel, and Linux i686 portable-core CI lanes are green on the frozen commit, with their SDL/device/runtime boundaries recorded separately.
- [ ] Smoke, benchmark, input, settings, camera, audio, bark, haptic-mixer, controller, rope, and the 600-tick deterministic load self-test are captured in every package; named-bench performance qualification is separately captured when explicitly enabled on that bench.
- [ ] The easy-run Linux bundle, launcher, QUICK-FEEDBACK.txt, CG-README.TXT, QA workbook/cockpit, Corresponding Source, SPDX SBOM, and SHA256SUMS agree on v0.0.3.
- [ ] Manual one- and two-human matches cover every map/landform, rising lava, bots, all eleven weapons, pause/results/restart, and clean shutdown.
- [ ] Compatibility claims remain evidence-scoped; no GPU, historical-OS, or untested controller claim is inferred.
- [ ] The accepted phase commits are merged to protected `main`, pushed to `origin`, and the final clean commit is used for any release candidate tag.
