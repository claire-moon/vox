DIGS legacy Win32 third-party notice
====================================

The legacy Win32 executable uses the repository's native GDI/WinMM frontend.
It does not link or distribute SDL2 or Lua 5.1.5. Lua remains in the matching
Corresponding Source archive as an optional desktop scripting component; its
MIT notice is retained in LICENSES\Lua-5.1.txt for source completeness.

The packaged controller mapping data is retained under SHARE\digs\controllers
for content-layout compatibility. Its zlib license notice is included as
LICENSES\SDL_GameControllerDB.txt. The native legacy frontend may choose not
to expose modern controller or haptic features.

VOX and DIGS are GPL-3.0-or-later. The matching legacy Corresponding Source
archive is published beside this ZIP on the GitHub Release page. Its
SOURCE-COMMIT.txt identifies the exact checkout used to build this addendum.
