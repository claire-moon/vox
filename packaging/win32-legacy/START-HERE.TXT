DIGS legacy Win32 addendum
===========================

1. Extract the complete ZIP with folders preserved.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click RUN-DIGS.BAT.

Keep DIGS95.EXE and SHARE beside RUN-DIGS.BAT. Do not move DIGS95.EXE out of
the extracted folder.

This package is a separate 32-bit legacy-host build. Its compatibility claim
is defined by the EVIDENCE folder shipped in the same archive. A successful
build and PE import audit are not substitutes for acceptance on the intended
period Windows installation.
