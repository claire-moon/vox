# Contributor covenant

VOX + DIGS is a public project. Participants must be respectful, technically
honest about evidence, and willing to document portability and provenance.
Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, credential exposure, and deliberate
license violations are not acceptable. Maintainers may moderate issues,
reviews, and discussions to protect contributors and users.
